Who holds what

  • StableX ledger — the truth of who owns what; every customer balance is a USDC entry here.
  • USDC pool — Base hot wallet + CPN operational wallet (+ Ndax for the market leg).
  • CAD pool — Fincra CAD wallet. NGN/GHS/KES/TZS pools — Fincra/Nomba wallets.
  • Bridge custody — transient USD/EUR/GBP until converted to USDC.
  • Rule: ledger sums per currency must always explain the provider pools; the audit trail labels every movement by source.
