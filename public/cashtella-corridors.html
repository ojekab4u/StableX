Cost per leg

  • Ndax (CAD): 0.20% per trade · 1.50 CAD Interac payout (≤10,000/day) · 4.99 CAD EFT payout · 0 CAD Interac deposit
  • Bridge (USD/EUR/GBP): conversion fee at deposit; per-customer account details planned before live
  • Nomba / Linkio (NGN): per-transfer fees per provider schedule
  • Base (on-chain): network gas only, typically cents

Benchmark: Pesa promo rate 976 NGN/CAD vs mid-market ≈943–952. Our CAD→NGN stack ≈ Ndax 0.20% + NGN leg spread + payout fee.

What each actor gets

  • Individual: one account → fund from a local rail → USDC wallet → send anywhere on the map → cash out to a local bank.
  • Business: same, plus virtual account details in their own name for receiving, and volume corridors for paying suppliers, staff, or partners abroad.
  • Providers are plumbing. Customers see one wallet, one send screen, one cash-out screen. Adding a country adds a provider; the app surface stays the same.
